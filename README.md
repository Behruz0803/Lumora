# BozorPulse — Wholesale Market Price Intelligence & Smart B2B Procurement

**BozorPulse** is a real-time wholesale market intelligence, 14-day AI price forecasting, and neighborhood group-buying (co-op) platform built for grocery shops, minimarkets, and HoReCa businesses across Tashkent, Uzbekistan.

### Key Features
- **Live Wholesale Price Index**: Real-time price tracking across Tashkent's major wholesale hubs (*O'rikzor, Qo'yliq, Sergeli Rail Depot, Bek Baraka, Eskijuva/Chorsu*).
- **14-Day AI Price & Supply Forecast**: 30-day historical price curves combined with 14-day directional forecasts and stock recommendations.
- **Smart Procurement Cart & Optimizer**: Automated weekly restock basket optimization comparing direct wholesale vs. neighborhood group-buying tiers.
- **Mahalla Co-Op (Group Buying)**: Consolidates orders from neighboring shops within a 2km radius to unlock factory-tier pricing and free truck delivery.
- **BozorKoz AI Receipt & Invoice Scanner**: Extracts structured commodity prices from handwritten bazaar invoices (`nakladnoy`), `Soliq.uz` fiscal QR receipts, and voice notes with statistical Z-score anti-fraud filtering.
- **Trilingual Interface**: Full support for **O'zbekcha (`O'ZB`)**, **Русский (`РУС`)**, and **English (`ENG`)**.
